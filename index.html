Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

What services does Panache Safety & Security provide?

We provide comprehensive security services, Health & Safety management, risk assessment, security consulting, trained security personnel, and 24/7 surveillance solutions for businesses, residential estates, and industrial facilities.

How quickly can you deploy security personnel?

We can typically deploy security personnel within 24-48 hours of agreement, depending on your specific requirements. For emergency situations, we have rapid response capabilities to address urgent security needs.

What areas in Nigeria do you cover?

We are based in Lagos but provide services across Nigeria. Our network allows us to deploy qualified personnel to various locations while maintaining our high standards of service delivery.

How do you ensure quality and accountability?

We have strong internal processes including 24/7 supervision, regular client follow-ups, performance monitoring, and continuous training programs. We also maintain open communication channels for feedback and immediate issue resolution.

Can you provide customized security solutions?

Absolutely! We conduct thorough risk assessments and work closely with each client to develop tailored security solutions that address their specific needs, challenges, and budget considerations.
